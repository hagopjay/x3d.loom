Physics music box

Pegs on the drum

Click a cell (or arrows and Enter) to set or pull a peg. It changes on the turning drum at once: a CollidableShape switched on or off.

What you are hearing

The drum turns on a standard X3D clock (TimeSensor and OrientationInterpolator). Each peg lifts a hammer's foot; past the peg, the hammer falls under gravity onto its bar. The note is that contact: PhysX reports the impulse, the bridge finds the impact, and an X3D SpatialSound plays a synthesised steel tine at the bar's pitch, as loud as the strike.

A cell flashes when its hammer actually struck. If a peg is set and the cell stays dark, the physics missed the note.
