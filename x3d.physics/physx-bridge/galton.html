Galton board

The board

These rebuild the board. Tilt and Shake change RigidBodyCollection.gravity while it runs. Tilting does not simply slide the bell downhill: sideways gravity speeds beads up between pins, so it mostly flattens (measured in Node: σ 2.5 bins level, 2.9 at 4°, 3.6 at 8°; the centre moved +0.45 and +0.23 bins).

Bins

If every pin were a fair coin, bin k would get Binomial(rows, ½). Real beads remember their momentum; the pitch decides how much: try 18 mm (beads zigzag, narrow) and 24 mm (beads stair-step, wide).
