The Hall, on Babylon

One X3D 4.0 file (14 MB, no shader in it): an octagonal hall with 88 PBR materials, 17 lights, and the real suns of two cities on 16 October 2026 crossing it on a 299.52 s clock. The file's 187 ROUTEs run the film; this page only starts, stops and seeks the clock and picks the viewpoint.

The Babylon engine

Our own X3D reader feeds Babylon.js __BJS_VERSION__ on WebGPU. It keeps the file's rules and lets Babylon do the rest.

Two suns, two cascaded shadow maps
Each DirectionalLight that asks for shadows gets a cascaded shadow generator (3 cascades, PCF), switched off while that sun is dark.
Light scope and units
13 of the 17 lights are global="false": each lights only the group it sits in, which is also what keeps every material under Babylon's light cap. Intensities are candela / lux, falloff is the glTF punctual model.
Two texture channels
The lunette patches carry a MultiTextureCoordinate and name the channel each map reads (baseTextureMapping, emissiveTextureMapping): carried as Babylon's second UV set and a per-texture coordinatesIndex.
Points without point sizes
WebGPU has no point size. The galaxy and the dust are camera-facing quads sized by the node's own PointProperties attenuation.
Blend modes
BlendMode (SRC_ALPHA, ONE) becomes Babylon's additive mode on the sun patches and the glass.
Animated geometry
Coordinate and texture coordinates move under 27 interpolators; the vertex buffers are rewritten in place, no shape is rebuilt.
Look
The Look button adds bloom, a light vignette and grain, nothing else. Off, it draws the file as authored.

Eight node types new in this port

Collision, CoordinateInterpolator, CoordinateInterpolator2D, Disk2D, Extrusion, MultiTextureCoordinate, PointProperties, Rectangle2D.

Use

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Space plays and pauses the film. The X_ITE button redraws the same file in X_ITE __XITE_VERSION__ for comparison. Not pixel for pixel: the gaps are listed in the run's GAPS.md.
