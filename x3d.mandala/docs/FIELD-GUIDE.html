01 / FIRST ENCOUNTER

Begin with the whole.

Watch the gold figure against the rose and lapis field before changing anything. The foreground is the canonical Sri Yantra. A quieter copy sits behind it; lotus petals, light ribbons, a frame, and distant points complete the mandala. Open Studio to separate these layers. Choose Awaken sound when you want the harmonic accompaniment; the page begins silent.

The supplied Babylon.mandala.md contained a synthetic-network mandala proposal and a Babylon.js starter scaffold. Its useful idea was the separation of simulated state, aggregation, and visual composition. This edition carries that idea into native X3D, adds audited geometry and a shared audio transport, and labels its sixty-four oscillators as synthetic. They are neither connected peers nor measurements of people or emotions.

Three different kinds of truth coexist here. The triangle construction is numerically audited. The ornament and harmonic score are authored. The oscillator field is a deterministic simulation. The interface keeps those claims separate.

02 / THE CHOSEN CONSTRUCTION

“Perfect” needs a definition.

A Sri Yantra is more than nine overlapping triangles. The selected planar family has a common vertical reflection axis, four upward-pointing and five downward-pointing maximal triangles, a shared circumcircle for two designated triangles, seven apex-to-base incidences, and twelve bilateral leg/base/leg incidences. The last condition gives twenty-four off-axis triple intersections [1, 2].

Those conditions permit a family of figures. This instrument selects a concentric Huet-family member: it additionally places the central triangle’s incenter at the outer circle’s center. Three parameter values are fixed; two remaining values are solved using 100-digit decimal arithmetic. Coordinates are normalized to outer circumradius R = 1. The bindu is at the common center.

This choice does not impose every additional condition proposed in the literature. In particular, central equilateralness and congruence of the largest triangles are not added. The certificate records the selected parameters so the drawing can be reproduced without claiming one universally mandated historical set of measurements.

FROM INSIDE OUT

1 + 8 + 10 + 10 + 14

The forty-three active chambers are triangular cells covered by an odd number of the nine maximal triangles. Their coverage depths are 9, 7, 5, 3 and 1.

WHAT THE COUNT EXCLUDES

Thirty-one intervening spaces

The full finite-segment arrangement has 74 bounded cells. Its 31 even-coverage voids include ten triangles and twenty-one quadrilaterals. Counting every small triangle would give the wrong total.

03 / THE GEOMETRY WITNESS

A tolerance is more useful than a slogan.

Open Geometry witness to inspect measured residuals and download the certificate. The independent audit splits the twenty-seven edges at intersections, walks the resulting planar graph, classifies coverage, and checks both chamber count and triangular shape. A perturbed drawing can preserve a count while failing the required incidences.

04 / THE GEOMETRY CONTRACT

The figure stays declarative.

The visible construction is native X3D: Coordinate, IndexedLineSet, IndexedTriangleSet, PointSet, materials, switches and transforms. X_ITE displays that scene directly. The mandala is not a picture painted onto a shader plane. Its lotus outlines, ribbons, fills, frame and points remain scene geometry.

Foreground and background use the same canonical coordinates through X3D DEF/USE. Each complete Sri Yantra layer may translate, rotate, or scale uniformly. These similarities preserve its incidences. Color, transparency, emissive intensity and layer spacing can change; individual canonical vertices do not wobble. The wider glow ribbons are presentation geometry around the audited centerlines.

The eight- and sixteen-petal rings follow the established surrounding form; their exact curves, spacing, colors and additional thirty-two-petal ornament are this instrument’s artistic choices. Their sampled curves are not included in the nine-triangle precision claim.

05 / SYNCHRONIZATION

One phase. Several rhythms.

At the default 54 BPM, one beat lasts about 1.111 seconds. A four-beat breath lasts about 4.444 seconds. The visual beat pulse, layer breath, halo periods, slow rotation, palette drift and sound derive from the same continuous beat position. Changing tempo alters future speed while preserving the present phase.

breath(b) = ½ − ½ cos(πb / 2)
coherence = | (1/N) Σ exp(iθj) |b is musical beat position. The first expression repeats every four beats. The second measures circular oscillator alignment; phases close across the wrap boundary remain close.

When sound is enabled, the transport uses AudioContext.currentTime. When muted it rebases onto the display clock without jumping phase. Soft half-beat plucks sit above sustained harmonic pads, a breathing low-pass filter, tempo-related stereo delays and a seeded room response. A short lookahead scheduler places audio events ahead of rendering; late callbacks skip overdue events instead of releasing a burst.

Pause freezes the transport and gates sound; backgrounding the page pauses it too. Muting alone lets the visual composition continue. Shared clock ownership does not promise zero speaker, headphone or display latency. The numerical geometry bounds do not describe audiovisual latency.

The roots are A at 110 Hz, C at approximately 130.813 Hz, G at 98 Hz, and a 108 Hz A for an A432 tuning reference. These are authored musical alternatives, with no healing or physiological claim. Listening level controls a bounded master gain; it is not a calibrated sound-pressure measurement.

07 / SIX INVESTIGATIONS

Let each variation answer something.

Separate the two witnesses

Choose overlay only, pause, and open Geometry witness. Then choose underlay only. Their appearance differs because of scale, depth and material; the underlying canonical coordinates are shared. A visual difference need not be a construction difference.

Change tempo while held

Pause at a recognizable petal alignment, change 54 BPM to 72, then resume. There should be no phase reset. The four-beat breath now takes about 3.333 seconds. Tempo changes duration without changing the defined geometric figure.

Distinguish geometry from projection

Compare frontal viewing with a small inclination and both layers visible. A tilted triangle can look asymmetric on screen while its model-space coordinates remain unchanged. Return inclination to zero before judging the flat construction by eye.

Find the circular boundary

Sweep oscillator coherence from loose to aligned. Watch the diagnostic order parameter rather than treating the control as a human “resonance” reading. Circular aggregation avoids inventing a large disagreement between phases just before and just after a complete turn.

Move pitch, keep the pulse

Awaken sound, keep tempo fixed, and compare A, C and G. The harmonic color changes while beat accents and layer breathing retain their timing. Compare muting with pausing to hear and see the distinction between silence and suspended time.

Test the essential composition

Use Stillness mode and lower overlay presence; then remove the mandala’s Sri Yantra layers entirely. Restore them one at a time. This separates the precise construction from the ornament that frames it and helps find a quieter, readable presentation.

08 / PORTABLE EVIDENCE

Keep the figure and its explanation.

X3D snapshot saves the scene’s geometry and current appearance. Sound is a JavaScript/Web Audio instrument rather than a set of X3D Sound nodes, so another X3D viewer does not automatically reproduce this score or transport. Save score records settings, beat/time, tuning and geometry identity; it is neither an audio recording nor a resumable checkpoint.

The certificate, construction script, measurements and independent auditor provide the geometric evidence. The validation record separately reports which browser, schema, timing and numerical checks actually ran. Draw counts and observed frame rates cannot establish performance on an untested GPU.

The reusable Loom principle: retain canonical data, keep the renderer declarative, give time one owner, and attach each claim to the witness that can support it.
