The three values of {CATEGORY} in briefs/author.txt, one author each. They are fixed here, before any kernel work
for court 5 begins, and were chosen to divide the X3D 4.0 components three ways, not from what the kernel checks.

SET a
BEHAVIOUR. Anything about what a scene does over time or on interaction: ROUTEs, event types and access types, TimeSensor and clocks, interpolators and sequencers, triggers/toggles/filters, followers (chasers/dampers), pointing and drag sensors, environment sensors (Proximity, Visibility, Collision), key sensors, Switch/LOD driven by events, Script nodes, animation loops, start/stop/pause timing, viewpoint binding, load sensing.

SET b
STRUCTURE AND DATA. A static scene that is wrong: grouping and transforms, DEF/USE, prototypes, geometry and its index and coordinate arrays, normals, units and angles, Inline and other addresses, metadata, profile and component statements, 2D and NURBS and CAD and geospatial structure.

SET c
LOOK. A scene that is built right and looks wrong: lights and their scope, materials (classic, physical, unlit), textures and texture coordinates and transforms, colour per vertex, transparency and blending, text and font style, fog, background, shadows, viewpoints and navigation settings, level of detail by distance, sound placement.
