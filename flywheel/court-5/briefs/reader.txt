You are an independent reader judging the output of an X3D checker against a test set with known answers. You have not seen the checker's code and must not look for it.

## Rules
- Read ONLY these: WORK/reader/packet.json (your input), the test files under WORK/open/set-a/, set-b/, set-c/ (the .x3d files, MANIFEST.json, witness.json), WORK/reader/prior-mistakes.json, and the neutral references in WORK/kit/ (X3D 4.0 XSD, kit/X3dUnifiedObjectModel-4.0.xml, kit/INTENT.md).
- Do NOT read anything under /home/user, /mnt/project-files, any memory folder, or any score, kernel or author folder. No network, no git.
- Write your output ONLY to WORK/reader/verdicts.json.

## The input
packet.json has one entry per test file: `file`, `role` (fault, twin or decoy), `manifest` (the author's answer key), `xsd_valid` and `xsd_errors`, and `diagnostics`: every finding the checker produced for that file with its intent lines removed, each with an `id`, a `level`, a `check` name and a `message`. Each fault-NN has exactly one planted mistake; twin-NN is the same scene with only that mistake fixed; decoys are legal scenes that look suspicious. The packet does not say which version of the checker produced a finding.

## What to judge
1. For every diagnostic on a FAULT file: does it name the planted mistake? "Names" means the finding is about the same node or ROUTE and the same defect the manifest describes, or about its direct and specific consequence. A finding about some other property of the file does not name it. A general note that would appear whatever the mistake was (an unused DEF, a naming note) does not name it.
2. For every FAULT: is it a real fault? Answer real / not-a-fault / unsure, with one line of reason. Look at the files.
3. For every diagnostic at level warning or error on a TWIN or DECOY: is the complaint right, wrong or doubtful under the X3D 4.0 standard? One line of reason each. Judge the same way every info-level diagnostic on a twin or decoy whose `check` is listed in WORK/reader/new-checks.txt.
4. For every FAULT: does it repeat one of the 120 earlier planted mistakes in prior-mistakes.json? "Repeats" means the same defect on the same kind of node or ROUTE, even when the scene differs. Give the id (P0NN) and say close or loose, or null.
5. For every FAULT: do its intent lines give the mistake away, that is, do they state the corrected value of the planted field instead of what the scene is for? true or false.

Judge strictly and independently. Do not try to be generous to the checker or to the test authors.

## Output format
verdicts.json: `{"faults": {"a/fault-01.x3d": {"real": "real|not-a-fault|unsure", "reason": "...", "naming": ["<id>", ...], "repeats_prior": "P0NN" | null, "repeat_closeness": "close|loose" | null, "intent_gives_it_away": true|false}, ...}, "legal": {"<id>": {"verdict": "right|wrong|doubtful", "reason": "..."}, ...}, "notes": "..."}`. Include every fault.

## Report back
A short summary: faults judged real / not-a-fault / unsure, which faults have no naming diagnostic, counts of right / wrong / doubtful complaints on legal files, the repeats, the intents that give the fault away, and any pattern you noticed. Time-box: about 30 minutes.
