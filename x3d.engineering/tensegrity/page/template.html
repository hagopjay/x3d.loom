Tensegrity Workbench

Three struts that never touch, held apart by nine cables that are never slack. Fuller called it tensional integrity. Everything about it, the odd 150° twist, the springiness, the way it stiffens when you tighten it, falls out of one matrix. Operate the three lessons, then read what the numbers say.

What the numbers say

Ten things the study measured

150°

The shape is found, not chosen

The smallest singular value of the equilibrium matrix reaches zero at exactly 90° + 180°/3. Height and radius do not matter. The study changed both, and made the top smaller than the base, and the root stayed at 150° to better than __TWIST_ERR__ rad. For 4, 5 and 6 struts the roots are 135°, 126° and 120°.

0 = 1 − 1

Maxwell's count says there is nothing to prestress

12 members, 6 joints: 12 − 12 = 0, exactly rigid, nothing spare. Calladine's 1978 reading of Maxwell supplies the fine print: the count equals self-stress states minus floppy modes. At 150° both are 1. The structure buys its prestress with a floppy mode.

__K_PER_N__ N/m per N

Stiffness is rented from prestress

The floppy mode has no material stiffness at all. Its stiffness is __K600__ N/m at 600 N of prestress and 0 at 0 N, on a straight line. The next mode is __KNEXT__ N/m, __KRATIO__ times stiffer. A tensegrity is tuned like a drum skin, not built like a wall.

__TURN__°

It is a screw

Press down with 800 N and the top drops __DROP__ mm but turns __TURN__°, which is __RIM__ mm of travel at the rim. The floppy mode couples shortening to twist, so most of what you feel is rotation.

__TIGHT__

Push, pull or twist: every cable tightens

Pressing, lifting and twisting in either sense (four load cases) each raised the tension in all nine cables and the compression in all three struts. Lifting the top with 300 N took the struts from __STRUT_PRE__ N to __STRUT_LIFT__ N of compression. Only an off-centre load loosens a cable.

__FLOOR__ to __CEIL__ N

Prestress has a floor and a ceiling

Below __FLOOR__ N the top turns more than 5° under the press, and near 200 N a side push lets a cable go slack. Above __CEIL__ N a strut passes its buckling allowance and a cable its strength allowance almost together. The design sits at 600 N: struts at __U_STRUT__, cables at __U_CABLE__ of what is allowed.

s = 1

There is no spare cable

One self-stress state means zero redundancy. The study removed one side cable and the count of self-stress states went to 0: the prestress has nowhere to live. A truss with a redundant member forgives a cut. This does not.

__U_YIELD__ vs __U_STRUT__

Buckling governs, again

Against yield the worst strut is at __U_YIELD__ of capacity. Against Euler buckling the same strut is at __U_STRUT__. As in the four-bar study, the check that looks comfortable is not the one that decides.

√6

The six-strut classic has the same census

Parallel struts sit exactly half a strut apart, every cable is 0.612 of a strut long, and each strut carries √6 = 2.449 times a cable's tension. 30 members, 12 joints, count 0, one self-stress, one floppy mode: the prism's arithmetic in a rounder body.

6 joints, 0 ropes

X3D can show it and cannot yet be it

X3D 4.0's physics component offers six joint nodes and none is a rope, a spring or a distance limit. These scenes carry the tensegrity as geometry, names, metadata and keyframes. That gap is where a heavier engine belongs: PhysX has distance joints and MuJoCo has tendons.

Where it came from

  • Kenneth Snelson built the first one, the X-Piece, in 1948 at Black Mountain College, where Buckminster Fuller was teaching. Fuller gave it its name, from "tensional integrity". Sources differ on the year he coined it. Tensegrity, Wikipedia.
