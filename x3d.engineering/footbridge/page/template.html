Footbridge Workbench

A 30 m steel truss footbridge, solved the way a bridge engineer would start: not "how strong is it under a full deck" but "where must the crowd stand to hurt each member most". The answer is a shape, one per member, called an influence line. Pick a member, see its shape, then walk a crowd across and watch eight members change from pull to push.

Stand one newton on the deck and note the force it puts in one member. Move it along and plot that force against where it stands. That plot is the member's influence line. A crowd is many newtons, so its effect is the area under the line where people stand.

What the mathematics says

__N_REV__ of 37

Members that change sign

Under a full deck no member reverses. Under a crowd that stops part-way, __N_REV__ do: the four diagonals and four verticals nearest midspan. The midspan diagonal D5 sees __D5_MAX__ kN of pull and __D5_MIN__ kN of push, where a full-deck analysis shows only __D5_FULL__ kN of pull. That is __D5_RATIO__ times too little, and no push at all.

line = shape

An influence line is a deflected shape

Lengthen one member by one unit, keep the rest rigid, and the deck moves into exactly that member's influence line. This is Müller-Breslau's principle. It was solved here from the transpose of the equilibrium matrix and agrees with the 37 lines found from unit loads to __MB__. Statics and geometry are the same matrix read two ways.

__GOV_U__

The end post decides

Every newton on the deck leaves through the two sloping end posts. __GOV__ is the most used member at __GOV_U__ of its buckling strength, so the bridge has __RESERVE__% of spare crowd load before the first member is over. Chords are sized by the full deck, webs by the partial one.

Strength governs, not stiffness

The full crowd sags the middle __DEFL__ mm against a limit of __DEFL_LIMIT__ mm. At a depth of one tenth of the span the truss is stiff with room to spare. Dead load alone sags it __CAMBER__ mm, which a builder would take out with camber.

__F1__ → __F1C__ Hz

The crowd is heavier than the bridge

Empty, the first vertical frequency is __F1__ Hz and passes the 3 Hz line. The design crowd is __CROWD_KG__ kg on every square metre. On the deck it weighs __CROWD_T__ t against __DEAD_T__ t of bridge, and the frequency falls to __F1C__ Hz, inside walking pace. The rule checks the empty bridge. A packed crowd shuffles and does not march, which is the usual defence, and it is a judgment, not a calculation.

0 kN

The member that carries nothing

The centre vertical V5 takes no crowd load at all. Remove it and the top chord's free length doubles: its slenderness goes from __V5_SL__ to __V5_SL2__ (limit 120) and its strength from __V5_CAP__ to __V5_CAP2__ kN against a demand of __TC_DEMAND__ kN. It holds no load and the bridge fails without it.

__DEPTH_F_LO__ to __DEPTH_F_HI__ Hz

Depth buys stiffness, not savings

From 2 m to 4 m deep the steel goes from __DEPTH_KG_LO__ to __DEPTH_KG_HI__ kg per truss, with no clear trend, because lighter chords are paid for with longer webs. Sag goes from __DEPTH_D_LO__ to __DEPTH_D_HI__ mm and frequency from __DEPTH_F_LO__ to __DEPTH_F_HI__ Hz. Choose depth for how the bridge feels.
