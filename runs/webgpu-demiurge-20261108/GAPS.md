# Gobbler demiurge on Babylon: gap log (8 Nov 2026)

Standing rule: not pixel perfect; log the gaps. Grades: C Confirmed (seen or measured here), L Likely (reasoned), S Suspected. Rig for every render claim: Babylon 9.32.0 WebGPU, SwiftShader, stubbed swap chain, requestAnimationFrame slowed, 960x540, correctness only, no timings; reference X_ITE 16.5.1 (WebGL 2, SwiftShader, full Chromium) on the unchanged original page.

| # | Gap | Grade | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | The tenders and villagers do not bend: HAnim joints turn (the interpolators run) but the skin is not deformed, so the figures stand in their rest pose while they orbit and walk their lanes | C | The body treats HAnimHumanoid and HAnimJoint as transforms. skinCoordIndex and skinCoordWeight are read and kept (FLAT arrays) so a skinning pass can be added. |
| G2 | No logarithmic depth buffer: the page asks X_ITE for one while flying down; here the depth buffer is a reversed float one, and the camera planes follow the page's nearDistance and farDistance (2.5e-5 at a villager) | C | The Air shader writes ordinary depth (its gl_FragDepth log output has no counterpart). No z-fighting seen at the plaza or villager stops; the real-GPU pass is open. |
| G3 | The Babylon frame is brighter than X_ITE's: mean RGB 1.3 to 1.9 times higher in six of eight views (table in RECEIPT.md); turning Babylon's tone mapping on changed nothing | C measured, L cause | The ported programs bypass tone mapping. Most likely cause: X_ITE clamps gl_PointSize to the adapter's maximum (the glow sprites in the feather view are far larger than a software GL allows), where the port draws the whole quad. Not tuned. |
| G4 | PSNR against X_ITE is 7 to 13 dB and means little | C | The scene runs on wall-clock timers (sway, orbits, tour, weave), so the two captures are at different phases; the contact sheet is the evidence, not the number. |
| G5 | The X_ITE reference for the village stop is black in this rig | C | The plaza and villager stops draw in X_ITE, the village-from-orbit one does not (software GL), so there is no valid reference for that row. The Babylon frame shows the planet, the cap and the village. |
| G6 | Sound (key M) not exercised | C | sound.js is Web Audio and does not touch the engine; the beds and the heartbeat tape are served unchanged. Nobody has heard them yet (also open on the original). |
| G7 | Hover cards, picking and the tags were not driven in the play test | C | They use the page's own projection maths on the camera pose the ProximitySensor reports, which the ladder test shows is right at every stop; a hover was not clicked. |
| G8 | Weave: the pixel dither is mirrored (WebGPU origin is top-left) and the face normal uses cross(dpdy, dpdx) (window y runs down) | C | Same density, same lighting terms. |
| G9 | The page's own loader still says "starting X_ITE" | C | boot.js is unchanged; the line is its stage name. |

The brain, Gobbler 3 and Odyssey bundles do not carry them. |
